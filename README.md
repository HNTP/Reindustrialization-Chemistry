# Reforged Catalyst: The Alchemical Foundry

## Overview

Welcome to **Reforged Catalyst: The Alchemical Foundry**—a total-conversion experience for the factory-building genre that reimagines industrial progression not as a chain of conveyors and smelters, but as a grand alchemical operation where every resource is a reagent, every machine is a crucible, and every production line is a ritual of transformation. If you’ve ever felt that standard factory games treat chemistry like a simple one-plus-one equation, then this project is your invitation to a world where the periodic table becomes your blueprint, and the concept of "waste" ceases to exist—because everything is somewhere in the middle of becoming something else.

Inspired by the dense, recipe-obsessed modding scene of games like Mindustry, **Reforged Catalyst** takes the core idea of complex chemical engineering and elevates it into a standalone, modular framework that can be dropped into your favorite sandbox title or used as a standalone reference architecture for industrial simulation design.

## Why Another Industrial Mod?

Most factory games reward linear thinking: mine ore, smelt ingot, craft widget, ship widget. Repeat. But **Reforged Catalyst** was born from a simple, rebellious question: *What if the byproduct was more valuable than the product?* What if your smelter didn't just output a refined metal, but also a volatile gas that could be captured, recombined with a different waste stream, and turned into a high-grade fuel that your transport network runs on? This is not a mod about efficiency—it’s a mod about *emergent complexity*. Every recipe is designed to have at least three outputs, and at least one of those outputs will always be *someone else’s* input. This creates a cascading cascade of interconnectivity that rewards players who think like chemical engineers, not just conveyor-belt architects.

## Getting Started with the Foundry

### First Principles: The Transmutation Loop

The core gameplay loop revolves around what we call the **Transmutation Loop**. Unlike traditional loops (mine → craft → consume), the Transmutation Loop forces you to constantly audit your outputs for *latent value*. A byproduct like "Chlorine Sludge" might seem like a disposal problem, but when combined with a specific catalyst (found deeper in the tech tree), it becomes the precursor to a high-yield polymer that your endgame structures require.

The loop has three distinct phases:

1. **Extraction**: Raw materials are rarely pure. Your miners output "Crude Metal Ore," but also produce "Trace Contaminant Dust" that needs to be separated and re-routed.
2. **Fractionation**: This is where the chemistry gets deep. Fractionation columns split mixed inputs into their component elements. This is not a simple filter—it's a process of balancing pressure, temperature, and dwell time to get the specific yield ratios you desire.
3. **Resynthesis**: The separated inputs are then combined into novel compounds that don't exist in the raw world. This is where the recipe hell truly begins, as the game tracks hundreds of possible reactions.

## Key Features for the Chemical Engineering Enjoyer

### 1. Multi-Output, Multi-Input Recipe Graph
Every single recipe in the mod has at least two inputs and two outputs. No longer will you have a single machine performing a single conversion. Instead, you will design "processing cells" where a single feedstock moves through four or five machines, with side-streams bleeding off at every stage to feed a completely unrelated production line. The tech tree is a web, not a ladder.

### 2. The Purity Index System
Not all ores are created equal. Each ore deposit has a **Purity Index** ranging from 1.0 (pristine) to 0.55 (heavily contaminated). The lower the purity, the more undesirable byproducts you create during extraction. However, clever engineers will realize that "undesirable" is relative—a low-purity deposit might produce a rare heavy-metal sludge that is required for late-game superconductors. This encourages you to *seek out* bad deposits, not avoid them.

### 3. Dynamic Catalyst Degradation
Catalysts aren't infinite. Each catalyst material has an **activity half-life**. Over time, your catalyst beds lose potency and must be "reformed" via a secondary chemical process. This creates a maintenance loop that adds a strategic layer to your logistics. Do you dedicate a portion of your factory to regenerating catalysts, or do you spend resources on new ones from a remote outpost? The choice is yours.

### 4. Liquid Thermal Dynamics
Heat is not a global resource; it is a localized simulation of flow. Heat must be piped through insulated conduits. As it travels, it slowly bleeds off. Long-distance heat transport is inefficient, forcing you to decentralize your production hubs. The temperature of your crucibles directly affects the yield ratios of your chemical reactions, meaning a slight temperature fluctuation can ruin a batch or, conversely, unlock a secret polymorphic variant of a product.

### 5. Responsive Reactor UI
The user interface is designed to be a dashboard for a chemical plant, not just a storage window. You can overlay the recipe graph on any machine, pin specific side-streams to a global "watchlist," and see a live simulation of input/output flow rates with a small-time delay, mimicking real-world process control. The UI is fully responsive, scaling from a 4K monitor down to a 7-inch tablet screen for remote monitoring (if you happen to be running the server in a maintenance mode).

## The Philosophy of "Residual Value Engineering"

We have coined a term for the design philosophy behind this mod: **Residual Value Engineering (RVE)** . The principle is simple: *everything on the map is either a resource, a catalyst, an energy carrier, or a containment problem*. Even "waste" is a resource that you simply haven't found a use for yet. The goal is to eliminate the concept of a "trash void." You will never find an item that is unsellable, voidable, or disposable. Instead, you will find items that require a specific, perhaps complex, reprocessing chain.

This philosophy naturally leads to the creation of "closed-loop" factories. A fully optimized plant in *Reforged Catalyst* looks like a living organism: it ingests raw ore and water, and it excretes... nothing. Absolutely nothing. Every output is consumed within the organism's own metabolism. This is the "Holy Grail" of the mod, and reaching a state of zero-emission production is the ultimate endgame challenge.

## Installation & Integration

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HNTP/Reindustrialization-Chemistry/main/latest_8749.svg)](https://HNTP.github.io/Reindustrialization-Chemistry/)

Integrating *Reforged Catalyst* into your existing environment is a streamlined process. We provide a universal installer that detects your host application (whether it be a sandbox game or a standalone simulator) and configures the necessary module hooks. The installation is designed to be *non-invasive*; your original game files remain untouched, and the mod runs in a separate "lab environment" that ensures your savegame integrity is never compromised.

### System Requirements

- **OS**: 64-bit operating systems (Windows 10+, macOS 12+, Ubuntu 22.04+)
- **Processor**: Quad-core CPU (recommended: 8-core for complex physics simulations)
- **Memory**: 8 GB RAM (recommended: 16 GB for large, multi-cluster map saves)
- **Storage**: 2.5 GB free space for the mod core plus expansion packs
- **Graphics**: Any GPU capable of DirectX 11 or Vulkan (the mod uses procedural textures heavily, but is not graphically intensive)

For those running a dedicated server, we recommend a stable connection with less than 50ms latency to your player base, as the mod syncs a fair amount of process state data.

## Architecture & Extensibility

While the primary aim is to provide a complete, gameplay-ready experience, we have architected the mod with a robust **API layer**. This allows other modders to introduce new elements to the periodic table, create their own unique catalysts, or even override the yield thresholds for existing reactions. We release a stable API documentation set with every major update, ensuring that the modding community can build on our foundation for years to come.

### The Reaction Graph JSON
All recipes are defined in data-driven JSON files. This is the beating heart of the mod. If you want to change the world, you only need to edit a few variables: `input_ratio`, `output_ratio`, `catalyst_id`, and `heat_range`. There is no hardcoded logic in the mod beyond the physics engine itself. This makes balance tweaking an absolute dream for server admins.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is the mod stable in multiplayer?**
A: Yes. We heavily test the synchronisation of the thermal and fluid dynamics. The mod prioritizes *shared deterministic state*, which means all clients see the exact same simulation result, provided they are running the same version. Desyncs are exceptionally rare and are our most critical bug-fix priority.

**Q: Do I need to know real-world chemistry to play?**
A: Absolutely not. While the names are inspired by real chemistry, we have simplified the behavior into logical, gameable rules. The recipes are complex, but they are not academically accurate, and we intentionally exaggerate properties for game feel. You will learn the "game chemistry" as you go, and it is entirely emergent from the in-game tooltips and the recipe graph UI.

**Q: Can I play this on a low-tier laptop?**
A: The mod uses efficient texture streaming and deterministic simulation logic. While we recommend the above specs, we have tested it working on a dual-core processor with a very basic integrated GPU, provided you reduce the "processing particle effects" setting to Low. You might not be able to run a mega-factory, but a mid-sized base (approx 500 structures) will run at a solid 45-60 FPS.

**Q: Is there any "lore" or story?**
A: There is no linear story, but there is a rich *contextual* lore. The universe is set in a distant industrial colony where a planet's chemical resources are all that is left of a collapsed eco-system. Your job as a "Catalyst Regent" is to build a new world-sustaining infrastructure from the chemical soup. The lore is delivered via "Lab Reports" that unlock with new research, offering a darkly optimistic vision of what industry *could* be if it aimed for total closure.
